Teacher & Admin User Manual

Robotics Lab Attendance Application Guide

1. Kiosk Mode & Badge Scanning

  • Automatic Card Reader: Plug in any USB keycard scanner. Tapping a student badge registers their check-in instantly with zero clicks required.
  • Linked Aliases: If a student has merged cards, tapping either their primary card or any linked alias card will check them in without issues.
  • Testing Without a Badge: Click the red scanner target circle in Kiosk view or press Alt + T to trigger a simulated new badge scan.
  • Auto-Focusing: Clicking anywhere on the screen refocuses the scanner input automatically.
  • New Badge Registration: When an unrecognized keycard is tapped, a registration modal prompts for First Name, Last Name, and Program. Upon submission, it registers the student and immediately logs today's attendance.
  • Full-Screen Lock: Press Cmd + Shift + F (Mac) or F11 (Windows) in Chrome to hide the address bar and keep students locked to the kiosk.

2. Manual Check-In (Forgotten Badges)

  • When a student forgets their badge, click Manual Check-In on the Admin Dashboard.
  • Choose their program. The dropdown dynamically filters out anyone who has already checked in today, listing only students who still need attendance recorded.
  • Select the student and click Check In Student to record their time stamp immediately.

3. Dynamic Multi-Program Management

  • Organized Program Ordering: Program tabs always appear in the consistent order of V5 $\rightarrow$ IQ $\rightarrow$ SRP $\rightarrow$ SRE, followed chronologically by any new programs added.
  • Add Custom Programs: Teachers can click "+ Add Program" to create new programs. Each program receives its own isolated attendance logs and student roster.
  • Program Swapping: In Registered Students, teachers can move any student to another program using Move Program. Their profile and all past attendance logs automatically transfer over.

4. Merge Duplicate Student Cards (Linked Aliases)

  • If a student has been issued multiple cards, click Manage ▾ > Merge Student Profiles.
  • Select the Primary Profile to keep, and the Secondary Profile to merge.
  • All historical attendance logs from the secondary profile are reassigned to the primary student, and the secondary card ID is stored as a linked alias.
  • Both physical cards remain active and will check in the same student seamlessly.

5. Attendance Records & Edvance Translation

  • Check-ins are grouped by calendar date and sorted newest first.
  • Multi-tap detection tracks multiple scans by the same student on the same day without creating duplicate rows.
  • Edvance Checkbox: Check "Attendance Translated to Edvance" once daily logs have been officially recorded in the school SIS. This greys out the day to prevent duplicate entries and persists across all browsers.

6. Student Profiles & History Modal

  • Alphabetical Sorting: Registered students are sorted alphabetically by last name, then first name.
  • Attendance History: Click on any student's name in the table to open their comprehensive attendance breakdown, showing their card ID, linked aliases, total sessions attended, attendance rate, and every logged check-in timestamp.
  • Edit Profile: Click Edit in the student table to update their First or Last Name. Changes cascade across the database, automatically updating all past attendance logs.

7. Inactivity & At-Risk Tracking (10+ Lab Days)

  • Distinct Lab Meetings: The app counts unique dates that students actually attended robotics lab sessions, seamlessly skipping holidays, exam weeks, and cancellations.
  • Status Badges:
    • Active: Checked in within the last 4 lab sessions.
    • Absent (5–9): Missed 5 to 9 consecutive lab sessions.
    • ⚠️ At-Risk (10+): Pulsing red alert for students missing 10 or more consecutive lab meetings.
  • Quick Filter: Click "⚠️ At-Risk Only" to isolate disengaged students in one click.

8. Admin Access & Manage Menu Safeguards

  • Admin Dashboard: Protected by the admin password.
  • Manage Menu: Consolidates data export, merging, and destructive actions into one location:
    • Export CSV: Downloads a formatted spreadsheet of all check-in records for the active program.
    • Merge Student Profiles: Combines duplicate card entries into one master record with linked aliases.
    • Delete ENTIRE [CODE] Program: Permanently deletes the program configuration, all students, and all logs. Guarded by typing Ronny is the Best!.
    • Delete [CODE] Logs / Delete [CODE] Students: Guarded by typing Ronny is awesome!.
  • Individual Student Deletion: Deleting an individual student cascades to remove their master profile, any card aliases, and all associated attendance logs.
