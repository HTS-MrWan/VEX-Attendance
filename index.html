Innovation Lab User Manual

Team Attendance & Multi-Program Operations Guide

1. Kiosk Mode & Multi-Program Keycard Scanning

  • Automatic Keycard Reader: Plug in any USB keycard scanner. Tapping a student keycard registers their check-in.
  • Single-Program Students: Tapping their keycard immediately logs attendance for their program in one smooth step.
  • Multi-Program Students: If a student is associated with multiple programs (e.g. V5 and IQ), tapping their keycard displays a prompt asking which program they are attending today. Only the selected program's attendance logs get recorded!
  • Linked Aliases: If a student has merged keycards, tapping either their primary keycard or any linked alias keycard will check them in without issues.
  • Testing Without a Keycard: Click the animated keycard in Kiosk view or press Alt + T to trigger a simulated keycard scan.
  • Auto-Focusing: Clicking anywhere on the screen refocuses the scanner input automatically.
  • New Keycard Registration: When an unrecognized keycard is tapped, a registration modal prompts for First Name, Last Name, and lets you check one or more enrolled programs.
  • Full-Screen Lock: Press Cmd + Shift + F (Mac) or F11 (Windows) in Chrome to keep students locked to the kiosk.

2. Manual Check-In & Audit Trail Tagging

  • When a student forgets their keycard, click Manual Check-In on the Admin Dashboard.
  • Choose the program they are attending today. The student list displays only students enrolled in that program who have not already checked in today.
  • Select the student and click Check In Student to record their timestamp immediately.
  • Event Tagging: The system automatically stamps these records as a Manual Entry rather than a card tap. This tag attaches strictly to that specific meeting, so if the student brings their card to the next session, their history accurately shows when they used their card versus when they forgot it.

3. Dynamic Multi-Program Management & Multi-Enrollment

  • Organized Program Ordering: Program tabs appear in the consistent order of V5 → IQ → SRP → SRE, followed chronologically by any new programs added.
  • Multi-Program Association: Each student can be enrolled in as few as 1 and as many programs as exist in the system.
  • Edit Profile to Add Programs: Click Edit beside any student in the Registered Students table to check/uncheck programs and save.
  • Add Custom Programs: Teachers can click "+ Add Program" to create new programs. Each program receives its own isolated attendance logs.

4. Merge Duplicate Keycards (Linked Aliases)

  • If a student has been issued multiple keycards, click Manage ▾ > Merge Duplicate Keycards.
  • Select the Primary Profile to keep, and the Secondary Profile to merge.
  • All historical attendance logs are reassigned to the primary student, enrolled programs are combined, and the secondary keycard ID is stored as a linked alias.
  • Both physical keycards remain active and check in the same student seamlessly.

5. Attendance Records & Edvance Translation

  • Check-ins are grouped by calendar date and sorted newest first for the selected active program tab.
  • Clean Check-In Display: Shows an interactive tap count pill for each student. If a manual entry was recorded, an amber badge appears beside the count.
  • Daily Taps Breakdown: Hovering reveals check-in times; clicking opens the timestamp breakdown showing whether each tap was via keycard or manual entry.
  • Edvance Checkbox: Check "Attendance Translated to Edvance" once daily logs have been officially recorded in the school SIS. This greys out the day to prevent duplicate entries and persists across all browsers.

6. Student Attendance History Separated by Program

  • Alphabetical Sorting: Registered students are sorted alphabetically by last name, then first name.
  • Separated History View: Clicking on any student's name opens their comprehensive history. If the student participates in multiple programs, check-ins are cleanly separated by program, showing distinct lab sessions attended, attendance rates, check-in timestamps, and manual entry indicators for each program.
  • Quick Program Filter Tabs: Inside the history modal, switch between "All Programs" (showing separated sections) and individual program views.

7. Inactivity & At-Risk Tracking (5+ Lab Days)

  • Distinct Lab Meetings: Counts unique dates students actually attended lab sessions for each program, skipping holidays, exams, and cancellations.
  • Status Badges:
    • Active: Checked in within the last 2 lab sessions.
    • Drifting (3–4): Missed 3 or 4 consecutive lab sessions.
    • ⚠️ At-Risk (5+): Pulsing alert for students missing 5 or more consecutive lab meetings.
  • Quick Filter: Click "⚠️ At-Risk Only" to isolate disengaged students in one click.

8. Admin Access & Safeguards

  • Google SSO & Faculty Whitelist: Only verified faculty Google accounts from the approved HTS whitelist can access administrative features.
  • Auto-Lock to Kiosk (30s Inactivity): If the admin dashboard is left idle for 30 seconds with no mouse, keyboard, touch, or scroll activity, the screen automatically signs out, closes open modals, and locks back to Kiosk Mode to prevent student tampering.
  • Delete ENTIRE [CODE] Program: Guarded by typing Ronny is the Best!. Students enrolled in other programs keep their accounts.
  • Delete [CODE] Logs / Students: Guarded by typing Ronny is awesome!.
  • Individual Student Deletion: Cascades to remove their master profile, alias cards, and associated attendance logs.
