Innovation Lab User Manual

Team Attendance & Multi-Program Operations Guide

1. Kiosk Mode & Keycard Scanning

  • Automatic Keycard Reader: Plug in any USB keycard scanner. Tapping a student keycard registers their check-in instantly with zero clicks required.
  • Linked Aliases: If a student has merged keycards, tapping either their primary keycard or any linked alias keycard will check them in without issues.
  • Testing Without a Keycard: Click the animated keycard in Kiosk view or press Alt + T to trigger a simulated new keycard scan.
  • Auto-Focusing: Clicking anywhere on the screen refocuses the scanner input automatically.
  • New Keycard Registration: When an unrecognized keycard is tapped, a registration modal prompts for First Name, Last Name, and Program. Upon submission, it registers the student and immediately logs today's attendance.
  • Full-Screen Lock: Press Cmd + Shift + F (Mac) or F11 (Windows) in Chrome to hide the address bar and keep students locked to the kiosk.

2. Manual Check-In (Forgotten Keycards)

  • When a student forgets their keycard, click Manual Check-In on the Admin Dashboard.
  • Choose their program. The dropdown dynamically filters out anyone who has already checked in today, listing only students who still need attendance recorded.
  • Select the student and click Check In Student to record their timestamp immediately.

3. Dynamic Multi-Program Management

  • Organized Program Ordering: Program tabs always appear in the consistent order of V5 $\rightarrow$ IQ $\rightarrow$ SRP $\rightarrow$ SRE, followed chronologically by any new programs added.
  • Add Custom Programs: Teachers can click "+ Add Program" to create new programs. Each program receives its own isolated attendance logs and student roster.

4. Merge Duplicate Keycards (Linked Aliases)

  • If a student has been issued multiple keycards, click Manage ▾ > Merge Duplicate Keycards.
  • Select the Primary Profile to keep, and the Secondary Profile to merge.
  • All historical attendance logs from the secondary profile are reassigned to the primary student, and the secondary keycard ID is stored as a linked alias.
  • Both physical keycards remain active and will check in the same student seamlessly.

5. Attendance Records & Edvance Translation

  • Check-ins are grouped by calendar date and sorted newest first.
  • Clean Check-In Display: Shows an interactive tap count pill for each student. Hovering reveals their check-in times in a tooltip; clicking the pill opens the complete daily timestamps modal.
  • Edvance Checkbox: Check "Attendance Translated to Edvance" once daily logs have been officially recorded in the school SIS. This greys out the day to prevent duplicate entries and persists across all browsers.

6. Student Profiles & History Modal

  • Alphabetical Sorting: Registered students are sorted alphabetically by last name, then first name.
  • Attendance History: Click on any student's name in the table to open their comprehensive attendance breakdown, showing their keycard ID, linked aliases, total sessions attended, attendance rate, and every logged check-in timestamp.
  • Edit & Program Transfer: Click Edit in the student table to open the profile modal. Update their First Name, Last Name, or choose a new program from the dropdown to transfer the student and their entire history.

7. Inactivity & At-Risk Tracking (5+ Lab Days)

  • Distinct Lab Meetings: The app counts unique dates that students actually attended lab sessions, seamlessly skipping holidays, exam weeks, and cancellations.
  • Status Badges:
    • Active: Checked in within the last 2 lab sessions (missed 0–2).
    • Drifting (3–4): Missed 3 or 4 consecutive lab sessions.
    • ⚠️ At-Risk (5+): Pulsing alert for students missing 5 or more consecutive lab meetings.
  • Quick Filter: Click "⚠️ At-Risk Only" to isolate disengaged students in one click.

8. Admin Access & Manage Menu Safeguards

  • Admin Dashboard: Protected by the admin password.
  • Manage Menu: Consolidates data export, merging, and destructive actions into one location:
    • Export CSV: Downloads a formatted spreadsheet of all check-in records for the active program.
    • Merge Duplicate Keycards: Combines duplicate keycard entries into one master record with linked aliases.
    • Delete ENTIRE [CODE] Program: Permanently deletes the program configuration, all students, and all logs. Guarded by typing Ronny is the Best!.
    • Delete [CODE] Logs / Delete [CODE] Students: Guarded by typing Ronny is awesome!.
  • Individual Student Deletion: Deleting an individual student cascades to remove their master profile, any keycard aliases, and all associated attendance logs.
